# 🎬 Marvel FanPage
### The Ultimate Marvel Cinematic Universe & Multiverse Digital Archive

## 🌟 Key Features

- ⏳ **Smart Countdown Engines:** Ultra-precise real-time countdown timers for upcoming blockbusters (*Avengers: Doomsday*, *Avengers: Secret Wars*, *The Fantastic Four: First Steps*, *MCU X-Men*).
- 🗺️ **Continuity Roadmap ("What to Watch Before"):** Visual prerequisite timelines with panoramic movie backdrops, importance badges, and direct canonical connections.
- 📖 **Marvel Comics Origins:** Authentic vertical cover art of historic debut issues (*Tales of Suspense #39*, *Journey into Mystery #83*, *The Incredible Hulk #1*), original creators, and trivia.
- 🎵 **Vinyl Soundtrack & Original Score:** High-resolution album artwork with realistic vinyl groove aesthetics and scene-by-scene track moments.
- 👥 **Cast & Characters with Real Photos:** Complete cast rosters and behind-the-scenes directors, producers, screenwriters, and composers.
- 🍿 **Streaming Availability HUD:** Instant direct links to Disney+, Prime Video, and Apple TV.
- 🌐 **Instant Bilingual Support:** Seamless real-time language toggling between English (US) and Spanish (ES) with zero page reloads.
